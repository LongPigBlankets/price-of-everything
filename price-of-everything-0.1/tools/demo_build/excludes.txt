#   - every data/starts/*.json except index.json, default.json, tutorial.json and the
#     DemoBuild.START_IDS starts (scripts/demo_build.gd), and
#   - every data/map_authored/*.json except the active document (active.txt).

# Rows inside data files (gated buildings, their recipes, Recycling research) are taken
# out by addons/demo_export/demo_data_filter.gd, not here.

# To bring a feature into the demo, delete its lines here and its gate in
# scripts/demo_build.gd, then run `python3 tools/demo_build/build_demo.py sync`.
# Anything new is in the demo unless it is listed here or gated at runtime.
