River hug
How hard roads are pulled to the bank, across the 4–24u COST_RIVER_HUG band. Adds a road along each bank.

Rivers run through the top-left and centre tiles — HSM edge midpoint → central-square corner → C0 → and out. Each rivered tile keeps exactly one crossing; everywhere else the banks are separate networks and road geometry is cut at the water's edge.

Overlays

One arc district per tile, and a district blocks its neighbours. Hung off the longest main road — half the tile where that road bisects it, otherwise a quarter. Ring spacing is always two building rows plus kerbs, with 2–3 radials out to the outer arc. Nothing else is drawn inside it.

Light green = half cross · yellow = C · red = L with 2–4 tanks in the notch · cream = rectangle. Annexes go on rectangles and Cs, detached by 5u. Five grey decorative outbuildings per tile sit off-budget, facing the nearest street.
