Building details panel: button study

The left column is the panel exactly as the game draws it now (Godot captures). The other two columns show the same capture with its interactive controls replaced by 3D models of the approved button types. They're lit from the upper left like the knob and gauge. Every replacement button responds to clicks.

Recommended mapping
  • Housed button: the main money-spending action (Upgrade)
  • Keycap: secondary actions (Change recipe)
  • Guarded button: irreversible actions (Sell, Demolish); click once to lift the cover, again to press
  • Embossed steel: close ✕ and back ‹
  • Annunciators: route choices; lit green = selected, amber = fallback, dark = locked
  • Selector plate (concept, not yet approved): Inputs / Outputs
Alternative mapping
  • Lit annunciator for Upgrade (lit = available)
  • Keycaps for everything else, including close, back and the Inputs / Outputs cards
  • Guarded button only on Demolish
  • Route choices as square keycaps: the selected one latches down and lights its lamp
