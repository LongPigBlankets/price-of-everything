Control block: keycap-led, eighth pass

This is the building panel's four main controls on one steel plate. The plate uses the Sunburst worn-steel texture, softened and lightened so relief shading reads, with the rounded, rubbed edge of the earlier Sunburst concepts. A lamp above the top-left corner grades the block from light top-left to darker bottom-right and casts its shadows away towards the bottom-right. The plastic is the cream of the approved keycap reference.

  • Buttons: the four white sections, all one width. Each is a raised keycap in the approved reference's cream, with navy text, sitting in a dark gunmetal surround. Its chamfered skirt catches the light on the top and left and falls into shade on the right and bottom, like the reference.
  • Icons: raised cream relief straight on the metal, with no square around them. They're built from the game's own icon art: the wheelbarrow, output gear, lorry, upgrade arrow and recipe glyph. The embossing follows the bottom-bar buttons: each face grades from light top-left to deeper bottom-right, and a dark, soft drop shadow falls to the bottom-right, including inside cut-outs.
  • INPUTS / OUTPUTS: raised white lettering on the metal, embossed the same way.
  • Upgrade: the arrow is a chiselled solid. Its base is the game arrow's outline, with the foot cut flat; its top is the same arrow a little smaller, every edge parallel, joined by a narrow even bevel. When the upgrade's research is met, it lights light green and only glows. Otherwise it stays cream, is embossed like the other icons, and the Upgrade button refuses the click.
