# Research presentation

Implemented in `research_panel.gd`; assignments are in `data/research_presentation.json` for all 248 research IDs.

- Goods always retain cream square plates. Buildings reuse `BrushedCard.draw_on`, the actual navy, brushed-metal renderer used for building cards elsewhere. Systems reuse the existing research glyph vocabulary.
- Cards are 350px wide, with the extra 30px reserved for the right-hand emblem gutter in a 110×80 icon slot. Subject artwork is aspect-fitted and centred within a 66×66 bound. Goods have a cream plate across the entire 110×80 slot. Buildings use the same embossed `KeyedBuildingIcon.keyed` texture and brushed navy plate as tile-view cards, without a grey tint. Larger cream emblems have navy backgrounds and cream outlines. Arrow pills partially overlap their emblem’s lower-left corner. Recipe symbols point right and stand alone without arrows or plus signs.
- Collapsed disclosure rows contain only the activity condition, including when tier/prerequisite locked or already unlocked. One line is 24px high; two lines are 38px. Long wording is compacted without changing thresholds, AND/OR choices or turn streaks. Full text remains in the expanded disclosure, followed by prerequisite and tier sections.
- Panel text is off-white, including locked cards and selected controls. Full condition wording comes from MatchState; the presentation omits “straight”, “consecutive” and full-capacity qualifiers while keeping durations and actual infrastructure utilisation thresholds. No research costs or completion thresholds were changed.
- Subcategory lanes have consistent column ranges across tiers. The `order` field sorts each lane/tier; adjacent positions were swapped to reduce prerequisite segment intersections first, then horizontal distance. Cross-category prerequisites and unlock IDs remain unchanged.
- Geometric audit: straight-line intersections between prerequisite endpoints fell from 35 to 4 across the displayed categories. This is an ordering metric, not a claim that all routed cable intersections are eliminated.

Validation: run `res://tests/research_visibility_test.tscn` headlessly for content gates, two-line fit, assignments, badge placement and layout overlap. `res://tools/research_layout_audit.tscn -- --after` writes layout coordinates to `/private/tmp/research-layout-after.json`. `res://tools/research_cards_shot.tscn` renders sample production cards; `res://tools/research_requirements_shot.tscn -- --tree` captures infrastructure lanes.

The contents/impacted-items panel and its tooltips have been removed. Pulverised Carbon Injection now requires 10 player-owned buildings actively running Steel Production recipes profitably; paused, idle, other-recipe and loss-making buildings do not count. There is no multi-turn streak requirement.

Research polish: columns now have 110px clear gaps, rows 100px and tier bands 120px. Cached routing prefers clear column gutters and penalises crossings with earlier wires. The focused audit compares the old router and new router on this spacing: 382→87 segment intersections overall, 59→9 in Chemistry; no card intersections. These counts do not include collinear shared segments. `tests/research_polish_test.tscn` checks artwork aspect ratios, binoculars, power mipmaps, deposit layers/hover blocking and routing. Power thumbnails and the bake script now use a 256px longest edge; the old 64px thumbnail was upscaled despite its valid mipmaps. Deposit pills render at canvas layer 0 below the HUD at layer 1.
