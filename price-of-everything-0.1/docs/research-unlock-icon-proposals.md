# Research unlock icon proposals — 6 September 2026

One proposal for each of the 248 current CSV unlocks, grouped by the revised categories. This is an art assignment list; it does not install or replace slot textures. Hidden demo nodes are included for future art planning, but remain gated in the game.

## Card treatment

- **Goods:** always a cream square background, including recipe outputs and storage cells. Centre the existing goods art with consistent padding; never place goods directly on navy.
- **Buildings:** use the existing building-card treatment: navy face, brushed metallic effect and metallic rim, with the building artwork centred. Do not bake a cream goods plate behind buildings.
- **Systems / people:** navy with a simple ivory pictogram. These represent capabilities, infrastructure or staff rather than a tradable good.
- Keep the base image dominant. Add a small corner badge for the effect: recipe (+), output (↑), maintenance (gear ↓), labour (people ↓), power (bolt ↓), level (II / III), throughput (capacity ↑), survey (binoculars), or price (coin ↑). For mixed rewards use at most two badges.
- Multiple subjects in a row are alternate candidates: use the first as the primary image and describe the full scope in the reward text. Avoid shrinking several goods/buildings into one tiny slot.
