# Market and supply-chain turn continuity

Market purchase and sale prices use white text with independently coloured direction arrows: green for rising, red for falling, including recovery towards the base price. Direction continues to use the market impact/recovery regime.

With the pointer over a price-history chart, Space presses the existing End Turn control. Text entry and modified keys retain their normal behavior. Refresh maps the pointer position against the updated history, allowing consecutive turns without moving the mouse or collapsing the detail row.

Supply-chain refresh preserves pan and zoom, including focused building views. If the focused building no longer exists, the graph returns to the whole-company framing. A bottom shortcut clears focus and returns to the entire company.

The end-turn dock has 5 additional pixels above its button; the button and bottom padding stay in place.
